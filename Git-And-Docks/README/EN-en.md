## About

I write software and games, make music, model in 3D and draw designs — trying to get a little better at everything. Self-taught in programming since 2011: it all started in primary school with mods for J2ME (Java) games on Nokia and Sony Ericsson keypad phones.

<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top"><b>Software</b><br><sub>Desktop and web: Electron, Qt, PHP, Python. If it can be automated, I automate it.</sub></td>
<td width="33%" valign="top"><b>Games</b><br><sub>Three Unreal Engine 5 projects in progress. Experience with Unity, Godot and Source.</sub></td>
<td width="33%" valign="top"><b>3D</b><br><sub>Modelling in Blender and Cinema 4D, rendering with RenderMan, texturing in Substance.</sub></td>

<td width="33%" valign="top"><b>Design and video</b><br><sub>UI in Figma and Adobe XD, graphics in Photoshop, editing in Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and CapCut.</sub></td>
<td width="33%" valign="top"><b>Reverse engineering and modding</b><br><sub>Digging into how engines and apps work inside: Source, Unreal, Unity, Godot, Blender, FL Studio, Ableton.</sub></td>

- **Working on:** three games in Unreal Engine 5
- **Learning:** Vue, JavaScript, TypeScript, C++, CMake, Qt, Lua
- **Looking for:** a job at a company that makes games or apps. Open to offers and collaborations
- **Goals:** join a team where programming is the job; release all my games; build things that help others create — a label, apps, frameworks
- **Ask me about:** anything, especially creative stuff. Happy to help with any idea
- **Areas:** frontend, backend, full-stack, game dev, mobile, 3D, ML, design
- **Game jams:** Ludum Dare 2023
- **Outside of code:** music, games, drawing
